Full-stack · PWA

Workout Logbook

An installable, offline-first web app for tracking sets, reps, weights, cardio, rest, and body weight.

  • Built-in 8-day training rotation that adapts to missed days instead of forcing a fixed schedule
  • Auto-starting rest timer with audio and vibration alerts, plus 1RM estimates against past performance
  • Cross-device sync with 30-day backups; optional Fitbit import via the Google Health API
  • Node.js
  • Express
  • Vanilla JS
  • Docker
  • Playwright
Senior design · Full-stack

Beehive Monitoring

A senior design team project at Santa Clara University: an affordable beehive monitoring system with data logging and abnormal-activity detection. I worked on the web app.

  • Worked on the dashboard: pick an apiary and hive device, then view graphs of temperature, humidity and wind speed alongside activity predictions and deviations
  • Worked on the Manage Apiaries page for creating, editing and deleting apiaries, devices and members, and a few other pages in the app
  • Part of a MERN app with JWT sign-in, role-based access (User / Admin / Creator) and Google Maps and remote.it integrations, fed by the team’s C++ computer-vision and Python ML modules
  • React
  • Redux
  • Material-UI
  • Express
  • MongoDB
