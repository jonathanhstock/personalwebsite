About me

Building software, end to end.

A developer with a systems-level foundation who enjoys turning ideas into polished, working products.

Background

I’m Jonathan Stock, a software developer. My foundation comes from computer science and engineering coursework at Santa Clara University, where I worked in C, C++, and ARM assembly on everything from data structures and operating systems to embedded systems and web infrastructure.

That low-level grounding shapes how I build higher-level software: I like understanding what’s happening underneath, whether I’m shipping a React front end, a Node and Express API, or a Python machine-learning pipeline.

How I work

I favor small, well-tested, understandable code and real, usable products over demos. My projects include end-to-end tests (Playwright), unit and API tests (Jest, Supertest), containerized deploys (Docker), and — where models are involved — honest evaluation against a baseline.

Beyond coursework I build practical tools, like an offline-capable workout logger and a laptop battery-health predictor.
