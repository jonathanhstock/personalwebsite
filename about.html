About me

Turning business problems into technical solutions.

An engineer who works across enterprise infrastructure, software, and the business side of technology.

Background

I’m Jonathan Stock, an Infrastructure Services Manager at Alameda County, where I architect, deploy, and maintain device configurations across enterprise endpoints with Microsoft Intune and SCCM, and build the packages and security-update policies that roll out to county departments.

I joined the county as a software engineer on CRIMS, an enterprise records system used by law enforcement, courts, and other criminal justice agencies, so I’ve worked on both sides: building the application and keeping the fleet it runs on healthy.

I graduated from Santa Clara University with a B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering (cum laude) and an M.S. in Engineering Management and Leadership. I’m interested in translating business challenges into technical solutions and enabling revenue through technical storytelling, which is why I’ve also spent time in B2B technical discovery.

How I work

I like to automate repeatable work and get to root cause quickly. I use AI-assisted log analysis and troubleshooting to diagnose configuration and deployment errors faster. In my own projects I favor tested, understandable code: end-to-end tests with Playwright, unit and API tests with Jest and Supertest, and containerized deploys with Docker.

Away from the keyboard I train mixed martial arts (muay thai, boxing, and jiu jitsu) and work on personal development through books and podcasts.
